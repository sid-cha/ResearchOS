03 / Climate-to-economy evidence requirements

CONDITIONAL CAUSAL LINKS

Pacific coupling: RONI measures overlapping three-month relative SST anomalies. Atmospheric SOI and wind fields are separate supporting indicators, not independent confirmation of a whole ENSO episode.

California winter: Need dated seasonal precipitation forecast, observed rainfall, basin-specific hazard and real asset geometry before financial exposure.

Peru fisheries: Need measured coastal upwelling, oxygen/nutrients, anchoveta catch and official quota changes, then fishmeal inventory and pricing.

South America agriculture: Rainfall effects differ by crop and region. Potash/fertilizer impact requires crop yields, import demand, supplier contracts and price vintages.

Point-in-time research: A 2026 fetch containing a 2015 value does not mean the platform saw it in 2015. Climate series can undergo later revisions; these must not leak into earlier backtests.
