# TradeScope feature status and where to find it

This inventory describes the source on `feature/tradescope-research-intelligence`, including the v0.7 Market Data Integrity baseline and the new **Research Intelligence Foundation**. The foundation is a bounded local pilot, not completion of the full ResearchOS roadmap. Source changes, final executed checks and provider verification are separate evidence; implementation does not certify an authenticated live feed.

See [the foundation guide](RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE_FOUNDATION.md) for setup, APIs, methodology and verification; [the requirements mapping](NEXT_RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE.md) records the remaining work.

## Reading an empty or unavailable result

- **Events ingested but no blotter rows:** check flags-only, symbol, type and search filters. TRADE BLOTTER or CLEAR restores the unflagged view.
- **Monitor Signal Monitor empty:** one trade for each of ten symbols does not provide twenty earlier trades for any one symbol. No flags can mean insufficient samples or no trigger; it does not establish low risk.
- **Research SIGNALS has no alerts:** load company research and evaluate the selected security. Ready false conditions and unavailable/warming conditions do not create positive alerts. This workbench is manually run, not a background subscription.
- **A company metric is missing/unsupported:** inspect its reason, period and usable sample count. SEC tags, matching periods, unambiguous shares and sufficient completed bars are required; absent inputs are not replaced with zero.
- **Chart has insufficient samples:** a populated ten-symbol ticker does not imply multiple trade points for the selected instrument. Historical daily research has a separate input path.
- **Event Intelligence or Filing Reader is empty:** use the relevant source/load action and inspect its status. Alpaca and SEC configuration are independent. A generic filing title is not a classified economic event.
- **Historical trades or outcomes have no data:** check a prior trading date, authorized feed, actual retention and matching stock/benchmark coverage. The application does not substitute demonstration data under a real source label.
- **Analogue list is empty:** no independent candidates may meet the bounded physical-feature query. Missing historical knowledge, verified exposures and unsupported outcome series remain unavailable.

## What historical analysis contributes

If a stock rises 3% while SPY rises 1%, a study can report approximately +2 percentage points of benchmark-relative performance. It cannot establish that the event caused the difference or that another similar event will produce it. The analogue method ranks physical similarity; its score is not a return probability.

Reliable forecasts still require versioned information available at each historical cutoff, verified exposures, appropriate licensed outcomes, chronological evaluation, simple baselines and calibrated uncertainty. Those remain separate deliverables. The pilot brings useful metrics, conditions, saved screens and historical comparisons forward while full Java integration and the broader roadmap remain open.
