# TradeScope feature status and where to find it

This inventory describes `feature/tradescope-monitoring-atlas`, including the v0.7 Market Data Integrity and v0.8 Research Intelligence Foundation baselines, plus **local monitoring, Market Atlas and optional Java research storage**. These are bounded local pilots, not completion of the full ResearchOS roadmap. Implementation and executed checks are separate evidence; neither certifies an authenticated live feed.

## Reading an empty or unavailable result

- **Events ingested but no blotter rows:** check flags-only, symbol, type and search filters. TRADE BLOTTER or CLEAR restores the unflagged view.
- **Monitor Signal Monitor empty:** one trade for each of ten symbols does not provide twenty earlier trades for any one symbol. No flags can mean insufficient samples or no trigger; it does not establish low risk.
- **Research SIGNALS has no alerts:** evaluate the selected security or inspect an enabled monitor's last run. Ready false conditions and unavailable/warming conditions do not create positive alerts. A monitor uses its copied criteria and requires the local server to stay open.
- **A scheduled monitor has not run:** inspect enabled state, next run, last run and scheduler health. Browser closure alone does not stop the local Node server; stopping that server stops schedules. An overdue persisted monitor runs once after restart, not once for every missed interval.
- **Atlas has no breadth/curve:** inspect eligible and excluded counts, source dates and comparison context. A missing/stale instrument is not counted as unchanged; incompatible or insufficient common dates do not form a normalized curve.
- **Java storage is disabled/unavailable:** Node-only research still works. Configure a matching private server token and local Java/PostgreSQL service, then check storage health before explicit synchronization. No token belongs in browser fields.
- **A company metric is missing/unsupported:** inspect its reason, period and usable sample count. SEC tags, matching periods, unambiguous shares and sufficient completed bars are required; absent inputs are not replaced with zero.
- **Chart has insufficient samples:** a populated ten-symbol ticker does not imply multiple trade points for the selected instrument. Historical daily research has a separate input path.
- **Event Intelligence or Filing Reader is empty:** use the relevant source/load action and inspect its status. Alpaca and SEC configuration are independent. A generic filing title is not a classified economic event.
- **Historical trades or outcomes have no data:** check a prior trading date, authorized feed, actual retention and matching stock/benchmark coverage. The application does not substitute demonstration data under a real source label.
- **Analogue list is empty:** no independent candidates may meet the bounded physical-feature query. Missing historical knowledge, verified exposures and unsupported outcome series remain unavailable.

## What historical analysis contributes

If a stock rises 3% while SPY rises 1%, a study can report approximately +2 percentage points of benchmark-relative performance. It cannot establish that the event caused the difference or that another similar event will produce it. The analogue method ranks physical similarity; its score is not a return probability.

Reliable forecasts still require versioned information available at each historical cutoff, verified exposures, appropriate licensed outcomes, chronological evaluation, simple baselines and calibrated uncertainty. Those remain separate deliverables. The pilot now includes metrics, conditions, saved screens, local schedules, requested-universe comparisons, historical event research and optional dated snapshot storage; full workflow migration and the broader roadmap remain open.
