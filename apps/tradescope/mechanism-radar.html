03 / Anti-Agent contradiction checks

REQUIRES INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE

Climate: ENSO is coupled ocean–atmosphere variability. A higher chance of California rain is not a forecast of an individual storm. Peru anchoveta and South American fertilizer channels require their own observable drivers.

Transmission: Each edge is conditional. Alternative causes, inventories, adaptation, seasonality, pricing and policy changes may prevent propagation.

Entities: Do not hard-code corporate names into the detector. Resolve affected assets and companies only after mechanism and geographic evidence with actual contracts and ownership.

Time: NOAA archival issue dates are not authenticated original first-publication instants. A report found today cannot create an earlier ResearchOS alert.

Research outcomes: Hypothesis paths have no calibrated probability or return forecast. Negative controls and out-of-time validation must come before statistical accuracy claims.
