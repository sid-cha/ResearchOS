Research integrity boundaries

Local hash chains can detect accidental alteration or inconsistent files, but are not independently timestamped or tamper-proof against an administrator rewriting the journal. An ALFRED historical as-of request made today is not proof the platform observed that information years ago. Confirm release vintages, source licenses and independently collected timestamps before using journaled data for backtesting.
